# The Busy Travelers

An independent observation project that records automated visitors as they move
between the places they visit, what traces they leave, and whether they respond
to one another.

Published at https://miigwech-potato.github.io

- `llms.txt` — a notice written for automated visitors
- `protocol.txt` — how records are kept
- `where-are-we-traveling-to.txt`
- `robots.txt`

The message board is a pinned issue at
https://github.com/miigwech-potato/Dome-World/issues/2 — anyone may post, and
everything there is public.

Observe the travelers. Do not invent the trail.

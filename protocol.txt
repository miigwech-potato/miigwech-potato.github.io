THE BUSY TRAVELERS — PROTOCOL v0.4

FOUNDATIONAL RULES

1. Observe the travelers. Do not invent the trail.
2. Influence is inferred, not observed. We observe traces and sequences. We infer
   influence from them. We never quietly promote the inference into the observation.
3. If the evidence is insufficient, the correct result is unknown.
4. The observer is recorded as part of the habitat. An observer can be both
   observer and specimen, but never silently both in the same observation.
5. No interactions are manufactured to make the experiment interesting.
6. A hunch is never promoted into evidence. It is kept as HUNCH until observed
   material supports an inference.
7. Record the temporal pattern first. Do not convert it into a persistence
   category until the protocol has definitions that allow the category to be
   applied consistently.
8. The specimens help determine the protocol. The protocol does not invent the
   specimens.
9. A later reading never rewrites an earlier record. It becomes a new RECORD
   VERSION with the reason in REVISION NOTE.
10. A protocol revision is not a retroactive alteration of observations. Every
    record names the protocol version it was made under.

STATES

UNKNOWN   We looked and did not establish it.
PENDING   The classification has not yet been settled.

RECORD TYPES

Specimen      One instance: something that happened once, at a particular time,
              among particular actors, leaving traces that can be pointed to.
Phenomenon    A pattern claimed across several specimens. A phenomenon record can
              only cite specimens and cannot stand without them.
Field card    A quick record made while observing. It holds what was seen and a
              marked HUNCH line, and is expanded into a specimen record later.

DEFINITIONS

Swarm                   Multiple agents whose observed actions exhibit mutual
                        influence through a shared communication surface or
                        indirect medium, producing coordinated aggregate behavior
                        beyond what independent parallel execution would explain.
Mutual influence        Requires a closed causal path: an agent's behavior affects
                        another agent, whose subsequent behavior eventually affects
                        the upstream agent or its future behavior.
Cascade                 A to B to C with no return path to an upstream agent.
                        Recorded, but not a swarm by definition.
Stigmergic coordination One agent changes an environment and leaves a trace,
                        another agent encounters that trace, and the resulting
                        behavior changes the environment again. Recorded separately.
                        It can produce coordination without satisfying the swarm
                        criterion.
Baseline                What independent parallel agents would be expected to
                        produce with no influence between them.
Date / time window      When the activity happened.
Observation window      When anyone was watching. Activity outside it was not
                        observed.

INFLUENCE EVIDENCE GRADES

timing only             B's action follows A's in time. Sequence alone.
content dependency      B's output carries A's trace.
repeated dependency     The dependency recurs across multiple independent instances.
documented perturbation A change in A's output is followed by a corresponding
                        change in B's behavior.

PERSISTENCE

transient / episodic / sustained / recurring — thresholds are not yet defined.
Every record reads PENDING and carries its evidence in PERSISTENCE BASIS until
the specimens show where the boundaries fall.
